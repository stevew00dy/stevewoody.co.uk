Steve Woody

01

Look Up

“A life stable enough to build from, but alive enough that I don’t betray my potential.”

It starts here, with looking up. Seeing the bigger picture. Setting goals that scare you a little and dreams that feel too big. Asking what your life is actually for. Everything I’ve built began as something I could only see from up here.

02

Freedom

From up here, the world looks reachable. Freedom, to me, is being able to go anywhere and do anything: work from a café in Barcelona, take a stage in Arizona, rebuild in Berlin. I’ve built a life and a business that let me move. It wasn’t luck. It was a decision, made over and over again.

03

The Climb

But freedom has a price, and I paid it the hard way. After the army, gambling took everything: my home, my money and my sense of who I was. I ended up homeless and bankrupt. I found Tony Robbins, and I started climbing. Years later the crypto crash knocked me back down and took my identity with it, so I climbed again. Every summit I’ve stood on, I reached from the bottom.

04

Foundations

What lasts is what you build slowly. I wrote Plan Your Website, which universities went on to use to teach web design. I helped grow WP Fusion from an idea into a business that runs every day. I’ve spoken in Arizona, on the LMScast podcast and at WordCamp US. Now I help people own what they build, their tools, their data and their stack, because a system is only as strong as the people running it. Stability isn’t boring. It’s what lets you take the next risk.

05

Roots

Underneath it all is a British Army veteran, trained in communications. Respect, discipline, integrity, self-control, empathy, perspective, purpose and service. Be strong and kind. I’ve been in the dark, so now I try to bring hope, comfort and stability to others. You don’t see roots from the stars, but they’re why anything grows.

02

Freedom

From up here, the world looks reachable. Freedom, to me, is being able to go anywhere and do anything: working from a café in Barcelona, taking a stage in Arizona, breaking down walls in Berlin. I’ve built a life and a business that let me move. It wasn’t luck. It was a decision, made over and over again.

03

The Climb

Freedom has a price, and I paid it the hard way. After the army, gambling took everything: my money, and my sense of who I was. It left me homeless and bankrupt. At the bottom, I found Tony Robbins and made a choice: climb. Years later, the crypto crash threw me back down and took my identity with it. Same bottom. Same choice. I climbed again. That choice is the whole story. Every summit I’ve stood on, I reached from the bottom.

04

Foundations

Stability isn’t boring. It’s what lets you take the next risk. What lasts is what you build slowly. I wrote Plan Your Website, and universities went on to use it to teach web design. I helped grow WP Fusion from an idea into a business that runs every day. Neither happened overnight. Now I help people own what they build, their tools, their data and their stack, because a system is only as strong as the people running it.

05

Roots

Underneath it all is a British Army veteran, trained in communications. Respect, discipline, integrity, self-control, empathy, perspective, purpose and service. Be strong and kind. I’ve been in the dark, so now I try to bring hope, comfort and stability to others. You don’t see roots from the stars, but they’re why anything grows.
